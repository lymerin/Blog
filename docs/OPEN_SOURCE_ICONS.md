# Open Source project icons

The icons in `public/icons/open-source/` are unmodified assets from the projects' official repositories, retrieved on 2026-10-13. They identify the projects; they do not imply endorsement. The original owners retain their branding rights and applicable asset terms. No logo was generated or replaced with the parent company's logo.
